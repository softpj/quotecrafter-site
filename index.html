Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. QuoteCrafter works without signing up. Your records are encrypted on your device.

How do I use QuoteCrafter on more than one device?

Turn on iCloud sync in Tools › iCloud sync on each device signed in to the same iCloud account. Your purchase follows your App Store account: choose Restore Purchases on each device.

How does the free trial work?

Start the 14-day free trial from Tools › Pro plans & purchases. Nothing is charged when it ends. Your saved work and backups stay available.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Without a trial or a plan, QuoteCrafter still creates up to three new estimates or invoices a month, and everything you have saved stays open for editing, printing and sharing. A Pro plan removes the limit and unlocks the Pro tools.

Can I import my price list or inventory?

Yes. In Tools › Clients, price book & job profit › Price book, choose Import CSV. Columns: sku, name, description, category, unit, price, cost and taxable; re-importing updates prices by SKU or name. Items appear in the estimate picker, where you can search by SKU, and they sync with iCloud like your other records.

Which payment methods can I offer?

Whatever your business already uses. In Tools › Customer payment methods you choose from the services available in your country: in Canada, Stripe, Square, PayPal, Wise, QuickBooks Payments, Wave, Helcim, Clover, GoCardless, Moneris, Interac e-Transfer, pre-authorized debit, bank transfer, card in person, cheque and cash; in the United States, Stripe, Square, PayPal, Wise, QuickBooks Payments, Wave, Helcim, Clover, GoCardless, ACH, Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, Melio, BILL, bank transfer, card in person, check and cash. Links are your own provider’s checkout pages, so fees stay between you and your provider, and PayPal.Me and Cash App links carry the invoice amount. QuoteCrafter never handles the money.

Can QuoteCrafter create a Stripe or Square link for each invoice?

Yes, optionally. Save a restricted key from your own Stripe account or an access token from your own Square account in Tools › Customer payment methods. QuoteCrafter then creates a checkout link for the exact invoice balance and can check whether it was paid, recording the payment with Stripe’s or Square’s reference. The key stays in your device’s keychain and is not included in backups or iCloud sync. Your provider’s fees apply.

Can my customer approve or pay online?

Yes. Emailing, texting or sharing an estimate includes a private link. Your customer opens it on any phone or computer, chooses an option, signs, and sends the approval back to you by email or text; opening it records the approval in QuoteCrafter, which checks that it matches the estimate you sent. Invoice links show the balance and your own payment options. No account is needed and nothing is stored on a server: the document travels inside the link.

Can customers request a quote from my website?

Yes. In Inquiries, tap Share request link and put the link on your website, social profile or email signature. Customers fill in a short form; it opens their own email or messages app addressed to you, so nothing is stored on a server. Open the link in their message and QuoteCrafter adds the request to your inquiries, once.

Can documents be in French?

Yes. In Business setup, choose Français for customer documents. Estimates, invoices, credit notes, receipts, emails, text messages and the customer page are then in French, with amounts written the Canadian French way.

Can I bill a job in stages, or export to my accounting software?

Yes. Start an invoice from an approved estimate and choose 25%, 30% or 50%; later invoices bill the remainder. Reports › Accounting software exports issued invoices as QuickBooks Online or Xero import files. Overdue invoices can get a late-fee invoice at a rate shown on the invoice.

Can I take a card payment on site?

Yes, with Square. On an issued invoice choose Take card payment with Square: QuoteCrafter opens the free Square Point of Sale app with the invoice balance, the customer taps, inserts or swipes their card, and the payment is recorded on the invoice when Square returns. Square’s fees apply; QuoteCrafter never sees the card. Works on iPhone and iPad in Canada and the United States.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, or use Manage App Store subscriptions in the app.

How do I back up my records?

Go to Tools › Backup & recovery and choose Prepare encrypted backup. Save the recovery key in your password manager. Without the key, the backup cannot be opened.

Do my customers need the app?

No. Customers receive PDF estimates and invoices that open anywhere.
