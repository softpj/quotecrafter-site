Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. QuoteCrafter works without signing up. Your records are encrypted on your device.

How do I use QuoteCrafter on more than one device?

Turn on iCloud sync in Tools › iCloud sync on each device signed in to the same iCloud account. Your purchase follows your App Store account: choose Restore Purchases on each device.

How does the free trial work?

Start the 10-day free trial from Tools › Pro plans & purchases. Nothing is charged when it ends. Your saved work and backups stay available.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, or use Manage App Store subscriptions in the app.

How do I back up my records?

Go to Tools › Backup & recovery and choose Prepare encrypted backup. Save the recovery key in your password manager. Without the key, the backup cannot be opened.

Do my customers need the app?

No. Customers receive PDF estimates and invoices that open anywhere.
