QuoteCrafter

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

QuoteCrafter is an estimating and invoicing app for independent contractors and small businesses. It is built so that your business records stay on your devices. We do not run servers that store your data, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. QuoteCrafter has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see your clients, estimates, invoices, photos, payments or any other records you enter.

Where your records are stored

  • On your device. Records, job photos, receipts and returned documents are encrypted on the device. The encryption key is kept in the device's secure keychain.
  • Optional iCloud sync. If you turn on iCloud sync, your records are stored in your own private iCloud database, which Apple provides under its own privacy policy. The developer has no access to your private iCloud data.
  • Backups you export. Encrypted backups are saved only where you choose, for example iCloud Drive, Files or another storage provider. Keep your recovery key safe: without it, an encrypted backup cannot be opened.

Documents you share

When you share or print an estimate, invoice or other document, it contains the information you entered. Where it goes after that is up to you and the apps or services you use to send it.

Customer payment links

You can add your own payment details or links from your payment provider to invoices. QuoteCrafter does not process payments and does not track whether a link was opened. Payments are handled by your provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

Optional checkout links: if you save a key from your own Stripe, Square or PayPal account, the app on your device sends the invoice number and amount directly to that provider to create a checkout link, and later asks the provider whether it was paid. The key stays in your device's keychain. Nothing passes through us.

Customer page (estimate approval and invoice links)

When you email, text or share an estimate or invoice, it can include a link to a page on this site (view.html). The document itself travels inside the link, after the # sign, which browsers do not send to any web server. The page shows the document, lets your customer choose an option and sign, and makes no network requests. Your customer sends their approval back to you by email or text; it is recorded only in your app. We cannot see the documents or approvals. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the request for the page itself (but not the part after #) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the free trial and the lifetime unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchases on the device using Apple's StoreKit.

Device permissions

  • Photos and files: only the photos and files you choose to add, through Apple's standard pickers.
  • Notifications: follow-up reminders you schedule, and payment reminders for unpaid invoices if you turn them on, are created and delivered on your device. Customers are never contacted.
  • Widgets, Live Activities and Siri: the Home Screen widget, the job-timer Live Activity and Siri answers use a small summary (totals, counts and a job name) kept on your device. It is never sent to us.

Deleting your data

You can remove sample records and delete invoices inside the app. Deleting the app removes the records stored on that device. To remove synced records, delete QuoteCrafter's data in your device's iCloud storage settings. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

QuoteCrafter is a business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Because we do not collect data, a change cannot affect information we hold about you: we hold none.
